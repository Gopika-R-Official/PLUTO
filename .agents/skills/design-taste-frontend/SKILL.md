# Design Taste Frontend (Taste Skill)

A system designed to replace generic AI defaults with intentional design decisions.

## Core Tenets
1. **The idea comes from the subject**: Ground the visual theme in the actual subject (Pluto's cinematic multi-intelligence feed, cosmic deep space, editorial curation), not generic category templates.
2. **Substance over restraint**: Richness comes from confident type scale, deep surface contrast, and real content, not frivolous decorative clutter.
3. **One live interactive thing**: Give the page one prominent tactile mechanism that visitors can interact with (e.g. topic filtering chips, live spotlight interaction, interactive trailer preview).
4. **No slop**:
   - Avoid generic AI patterns: centered 3-line headlines with pill tags above them, rows of identical feature cards, excessive decorative gradients.
   - Keep headlines under 2 lines at any viewport width (~32-40 characters).
   - Ensure high contrast and scannability.
5. **Real-world verification**: Inspect the layout across desktop, tablet, and mobile viewports.
